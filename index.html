Low-Stress Seismic Tomography in Earth's Critical Zone

Aug 2025 – Present

Independently designed and built a low-stress seismic tomography apparatus to establish ground-truth seismic wave velocities in the critical zone — Earth's thin outer shell where rock meets life. Developed custom Python pipelines for processing seismic arrivals, computing multi-media wave velocities, and generating high-resolution tomographic models. Designed cross-borehole tomography and soil-compaction instrumentation to advance effective medium theory.

Surface Analysis of Venus — NASA VenSAR Mission

Nov 2024 – Aug 2025

Collaborated on target selection and geospatial analysis for NASA's upcoming VenSAR radar mission. Contributed to surface region analysis for one of the first dedicated Venus radar mapping campaigns in decades.
